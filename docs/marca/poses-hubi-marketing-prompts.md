# Prompts de las poses de marketing de Hubi

Se usó ImageGen integrado con el original de cada tema y fondo transparente. Cada variante se generó por separado y se comprobó la alineación de los ojos y la posición de la boca antes de exportar.

Referencia `originales/volumen-claro.png`.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Create one high-end Hubly mascot asset (campana) by editing the reference image, with actual transparent background. The given Hubi reference is the actual target asset. LOCK THE ENTIRE HEAD. Leave the head's position, outline, ears, eyes, mouth, angles, light and shading exactly as shown, as if the entire head region was masked and unable to change. Preserve the graphite charcoal material, ivory facial marks and blue right ear in the reference exactly.
Crucial face quality: front-on symmetrical face with head level and original wide, low proportions. Exactly two identical slim vertical capsule eyes; straight upright axes, equal height and size, on one perfectly horizontal baseline, evenly spaced from the centerline. Keep the original tiny continuous closed lowercase w smile, centered between eyes and below them, with its original rounded curves and even weight. Keep the clear negative space of the cheeks. Do not redraw, tilt, widen or distort any facial feature. No wink, brows, nose, whiskers, open mouth, tongue, cheeks, pupils or teeth.

Hubi holds it in one lifted rounded mitten paw and the other front paw is raised a little as if introducing a campaign. Both round feet remain seated.
Keep the original calm soft-touch 3D graphite and blue art direction. The lower-body prop is tidy and secondary, same soft matte material as the original. No floating icons, decorative effects, text, borders, shadows outside silhouette or added background.
```
