# Prompts de la colección de Hubi

Se usó ImageGen integrado con fondo transparente. Cada pose y tema se generaron por separado.

### correo · claro

Referencia `originales/volumen-claro.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one production-ready Hubly web mascot illustration (correo), isolated transparent PNG. Image 1 is the authoritative full-body Hubi character and lighting reference. Repose exactly that mascot. A calm 'check your email' pose. Seated with body rotated slightly toward viewer left, Hubi hugs one softly rounded pale envelope across the upper belly with both paws, below the face, showing a recognizable simple V flap and a small blue circular seal. Keep the face front-on. Match the reference's charcoal-graphite body and face, white eyes and tiny white w mouth, and saturated cobalt ear.
Preserve the identity very strictly: head wide and low (about 1.45 times wider than tall INCLUDING ears), broad horizontal cheeks, flat-ish forehead and gently flat broad chin, big compared with the small bean body, two small pointed rounded triangle ears at the outer ends and exactly one blue ear on the viewer's right. Tiny vertical capsule eyes with no pupils and tiny w mouth, no nose or whiskers, rounded paws with no fingers, curled tail on viewer's right, minimal soft matte 3D finish with lighting from upper left. Preserve the relative size of face details and body proportions. No human-like limbs, no extra markings. Single centered square composition, entire silhouette including tail and prop visible, about 10% clear margin on all edges. Actual fully transparent background, no ground plane, no cast shadow outside silhouette, no border, no text, no marks.
```
