# Prompts de las poses de Hubi

Se usó ImageGen integrado con fondo transparente en las seis llamadas. Cada pose y tema se generaron por separado.

### bienvenida · claro

Referencia: `originales/volumen-claro.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one production-ready Hubly web mascot illustration (bienvenida), isolated transparent PNG. Image 1 is the authoritative full-body Hubi character and lighting reference. Repose exactly that mascot. A warm welcome after signing up. Hubi stays seated facing camera and raises one small rounded mitten paw on the viewer's left in a friendly wave beside the lower cheek. Other paw down. No props or confetti. Match the reference's charcoal-graphite body and face, white eyes and tiny white w mouth, and saturated cobalt ear.
Preserve the identity very strictly: head wide and low (about 1.4 times wider than tall INCLUDING ears), broad horizontal cheeks, flat-ish forehead and gently flat broad chin, big compared with the small bean body, two small pointed rounded triangle ears at the outer ends and exactly one blue ear on the viewer's right. Tiny vertical capsule eyes with no pupils and tiny w mouth, no nose or whiskers, rounded paws with no fingers, curled tail on viewer's right, minimal soft matte 3D finish with lighting from upper left. Preserve size of facial features relative to head and proportions of body like the reference. No human-like limbs, no smile change, no extra markings. Single centered square composition, entire silhouette including tail and prop visible, about 10% clear margin on all edges. Actual fully transparent background, no ground plane, no cast shadow outside silhouette, no border, no text, no marks.
```
